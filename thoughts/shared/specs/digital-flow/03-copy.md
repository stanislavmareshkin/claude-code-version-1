## Deliverable 3: Conversion Copywriter

---

## Voice & Tone

**Brand voice**: Confident, clear, professional — never salesy or hyperbolic.
**Tone**: Authoritative but approachable. Think "smart colleague explaining a great tool."
**Avoid**: Buzzwords ("revolutionize", "game-changer"), vague superlatives ("best-in-class"), exclamation marks.
**Embrace**: Specificity, active voice, short sentences, benefit-over-feature framing.

### FAQ Section

**Q: Is there really a free plan?**
> Yes. The Starter plan is free forever for up to 5 team members with core features. No credit card required.

**Q: What happens after my 14-day trial?**
> You keep access to your workspace. If you don't upgrade, you'll be moved to the Starter plan — no data lost.

**Q: Can I switch plans at any time?**
> Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

**Q: Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?**
> Yes. We offer 50% off Pro plans for verified nonprofits and educational institutions. Contact us for details.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
> We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and offer invoice-based billing for Enterprise plans.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
> All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We're SOC 2 Type II compliant and undergo regular security audits. See our security page for details.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
> Yes. You can export all your data at any time in standard formats (CSV, JSON). Your data is always yours.
